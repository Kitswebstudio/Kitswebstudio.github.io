A Chapeltown tiler with big-site experience

Photo of Brenden at work

Bailey Tiling and Underfloor Heating is run by Brenden Bailey, who has worked as a professional tiler in Chapeltown since 2006. He trained with Professional Ceramics in London.

Brenden has tiled homes across Sheffield and Yorkshire, and worked on commercial projects across the country, from Heathrow and Stansted to hotels, restaurants and the London Olympic Village.

As well as wall and floor tiling, he fits wet rooms and electric underfloor heating, and can supply tiles, adhesives, grouts, tanking kits, bathroom furniture, shower screens and showers. When a job needs plastering, plumbing or electrics, he brings in trusted tradespeople so you still have one person to deal with.

Preferred partners: Topps, Warmup and Nick Firth.
