Probate and estate administration

Grant of probate only

£600 plus VAT where no inheritance tax is payable. Third-party costs are extra: court fee £155, will search £118, asset search £186, money laundering check £6.30, office copies £3, bankruptcy search £2, and £1.50 per extra copy of the grant. Collecting and sharing out the estate is charged at the hourly rate.

Full estate administration

Charged on time spent and the size of the estate, at up to £200 an hour plus VAT. As a guide, about 1% of the estate's value (not counting the home) and 0.25% of the value of any home. Selling or transferring a property is quoted separately. Most estates take 3 to 12 months.
