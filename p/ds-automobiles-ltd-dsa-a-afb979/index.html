Book my MOT

For cars in England, Scotland and Wales. A new car needs its first MOT by its third birthday. You can have the test up to a month (minus a day) early and keep the same renewal date.

What the MOT looks at

  • Lights, indicators and the horn
  • Brakes, steering and suspension
  • Tyres and wheels
  • Exhaust and emissions
  • Seatbelts, seats and mirrors
  • Windscreen, wipers and washers
  • Body and structure, fuel system, registration plates
