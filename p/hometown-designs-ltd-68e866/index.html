About us

A family-run showroom in Rotherham

HomeTown Designs is a family-run, independent business with over 30 years of experience transforming homes across Rotherham, Sheffield, Doncaster, Barnsley and South Yorkshire.

No two of our designs are the same. We start with a free home visit and survey, plan the room around the way you live, and fit it with our own tradespeople, so you never have to chase separate builders, tilers, electricians and gas engineers.

Our showroom off Bawtry Road has 25 room settings: kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and studies, with furniture, fixtures and lighting you can see and touch.
