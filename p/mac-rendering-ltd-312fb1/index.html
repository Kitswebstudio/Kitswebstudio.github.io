How a job runs

No day rates and no subcontractors. Chris oversees every job, with our own employed team.

  1. Free survey

    We visit within a few days, bring sample panels and take photos for the scaffolders.

  2. Written quote

    A detailed quotation follows within a few days, with the system, colour and guarantees set out.

  3. The work

    Scaffold up, windows, doors and floors protected, mesh in the base coat, then the finish.

  4. Clean and clear

    Sheets off, windows cleaned, skip collected and scaffold down.

About

A Sheffield render company that does one job at a time

Chris McVann started plastering in 2004 and moved the business's focus to rendering around 2011. MAC Rendering Ltd is now based on the Neepsend Industrial Estate in Sheffield.

We complete rendering projects one at a time. We don't subcontract: Chris oversees the work, with employed staff and local apprentices.

All staff are insured, hold CSCS cards and get regular health and safety training. We spray with PFT machines and use quality beading and mesh on every job.
