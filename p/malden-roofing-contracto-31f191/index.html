Warm roof or cold roof?

Two ways to insulate a flat roof. We build both, and we'll recommend one after the survey.

Warm roof

The insulation sits on top of the deck, under the waterproofing. The deck stays warm and dry, and there's no need to vent the roof.

Cold roof

The insulation sits between the joists, under the deck, with an air gap above it that must be ventilated. Common on older homes and garages.

Pitched roofs too
