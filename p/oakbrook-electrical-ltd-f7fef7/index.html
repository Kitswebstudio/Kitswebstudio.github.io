We're bulb and battery specialists. Bring the old one in and we'll match it. If it's not in stock, we'll do our best to source it for you.

Which bulb fits?

Check the end of your old bulb. These are the four fittings we're asked about most.

B22 bayonet

Push in and twist. Two small pins on the side. The UK's most common fitting.

E27 screw

A large screw thread. Common on lamps and newer pendant fittings.

E14 small screw

A smaller thread, often on candle bulbs, chandeliers and fridges.

GU10 spotlight

Two thick pins. Push in and twist. Used in most kitchen and bathroom spotlights.

Not sure? Bring the old bulb in, or send a photo when you ask us to put one by.
