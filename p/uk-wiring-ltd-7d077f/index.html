About UK Wiring

A Sheffield and Dronfield based electrical contractor working on homes, businesses, care homes and industrial sites.

We're a NICEIC registered electrical contractor and a Part P registered installer, based on Ecclesall Road in Sheffield. We look after homes across Sheffield and the surrounding areas, care homes and supported living across Yorkshire, and commercial projects across the UK.

Whatever the size of the job, we aim to be polite, reliable and professional, and to fit around what you need. Every installation is tested and certified when it's finished.
